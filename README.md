## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a software engineer with a background in **backend systems, business software, databases, and long-term production maintenance**.

My earlier professional work included backend development in **ISP / broadband services** and **cross-border e-commerce**, followed by many years of independently building and maintaining production software.

Working with software over long periods has gradually shifted my interests beyond application logic.

I am increasingly interested in understanding how software behaves when systems become concurrent, networked, distributed, delayed, partially unavailable, or inconsistent.

My current work centres around:

**Systems · Computer Networks · Concurrency · Distributed Computing**

The technologies used may vary depending on the problem being explored.

## 🔬 Experimental Approach

I prefer to approach systems questions through a simple cycle:

```text
question
   ↓
build
   ↓
observe
   ↓
break
   ↓
measure
   ↓
explain
   ↓
refine
```

An experiment is more useful when it can answer:

* What question was being tested?
* What assumptions were made?
* What conditions were controlled?
* What actually happened?
* Can the result be reproduced?
* What remains unexplained?
* Where are the limitations?

A failed assumption can be as useful as a successful implementation.

## 🎯 Engineering Perspective

I value systems that can be understood rather than merely assembled.

That means looking beyond the happy path and asking what happens when assumptions stop holding.

Reliable software is rarely defined only by what happens when everything works.

It is also defined by what happens when something does not.
